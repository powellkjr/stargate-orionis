# Faction Campaign and Nine Endings — Reconciliation

## Authority and scope

Records the latest user-supplied faction campaign handoff. It supplements
[campaign context](campaign-context-reconciliation.md), not the Profession,
Haven, World Map, Theory or physical-instance contracts. These are campaign
design requirements, not implemented mechanics. Latest explicit direction
supersedes older campaign names; established domain architecture still applies.

The [central-threat questions](central-threat-reconciliation.md) remain on the
deferred checklist. Do not resolve them as a prerequisite to recording more
incoming design. Detailed late-game faction opposition is deliberately excluded:
do not infer Act V enemies, mandatory faction wars or final confrontations.

## Accepted campaign matrix

| Faction | Path | Strategic direction | Still to author |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scions | The Promise | Recover and restore a specific lost Ancestral solution through records, sites and addresses. | Exact weapon/system, deployment and durable victory. |
| Scions | The Chorus | Coordinate the routed Gate network as an anti-threat system. | Protocol, infrastructure, activation, costs and effects. |
| Scions | The Vigil | Restore dedicated Ancestral Sentinel defense architecture. | Control, repair, production/deployment and coverage. |
| Concord | Emergence | Transform humans through a Moy'na-regulated reconstruction process. Each generation requires treatment; children are not automatically Emerged. | Capabilities, costs, consent and implementation. |
| Concord | Resurgence | Activate a suppressed Moy'na developmental stage; the bonded pair remain distinct but the bond becomes permanent. | Final capabilities and execution requirements. |
| Concord | Convergence | Create a genuine human–Moy'na hybrid, not merely a stronger bond. | Whether existing pairs merge or new individuals are created; inheritance and transfer. |
| CLP | Choice | Interoperable rights, credentials and institutions support a federation/republic-like civilization and coordinated response. | Operational mechanism producing genuine victory. |
| CLP | Logistics | Build sustainable human-controlled transport, then selectively disable relays to fragment galaxy-scale Gate connectivity. | Cut sets, shipping readiness, local survival and remaining infestations. |
| CLP | Protection | Achieve total military victory through finite physical, industrial and strategic capabilities. | Campaign objectives, reservoir clearance and durable victory. |

Exactly nine primary endings must be viable on their own terms. No hidden correct
faction, mandatory golden coalition or SGC-only true solution. Cooperation can
improve cost, safety, stability and humanity; it does not certify an otherwise
false ending. Endings follow accumulated commitments, not one final dialogue
choice. Each must describe both victory and the civilization produced by it.

## Conflict and compatibility checklist

Checked entries mean the reconciliation is recorded, not that a mechanic exists.

- [x] **Awakening vs Resurgence:** use **Resurgence** for the Concord campaign
  path. The latest handoff supersedes the earlier program label. Do not rename
  unrelated uses of awakening or compatibility identifiers without inspection.
- [x] **Broad Scion site consolidation vs three named paths:** Promise, Chorus
  and Vigil now distinguish the solutions. The earlier approximately-nine-site
  idea remains a working campaign target, not a requirement that every path
  uses the same sites or all nine locations.
- [x] **Scion inheritance vs manufacturing:** operating/restoring inherited
  technology does not grant its manufacturing Pattern or industrial replacement
  capability. Vigil may pursue production capability, but this is a campaign
  achievement needing authored prerequisites, not a starting Scion power.
- [x] **Faction victories vs convenient threat weaknesses:** preserve all nine
  viable strategies and stress-test underdeveloped biology and strategy together.
  Biological advantages are not blanket immunity; military victory requires a
  finite physically defeatable threat. Isolation alone does not clear a locally
  sustainable infestation. These requirements must be reconciled, not used to
  declare a faction secretly wrong.
- [x] **Choice vs automatic diplomatic win:** integration is a legitimate
  strategic direction, but does not automatically destroy threat organisms.
  Its operational victory mechanism remains open.
- [x] **Logistics vs destroying every Gate:** prefer the repository's routed
  network model. Disable selected relays based on actual topology; do not invent
  universal critical hubs or erase all local Gate functionality by declaration.
- [ ] **Eclipse / The Long Way Home reference:** the handoff ties Logistics to
  them, but their precise mechanics and the starting SGC premise remain unresolved
  in the reconciliation. Routing is established independently; this reference
  does not settle buffered transit, root transit or historical interruption.
- [x] **Concord government:** one faction-wide Queen plus President; each Haven
  has Princess plus Governor. Retain the permanent human Senate structure.
- [x] **Nest evacuation vs relocation:** mature Princess nests are effectively
  immovable ecosystems. Long-term new-nest establishment is not evacuation.
  Logistics does not require relocating every Concord population; author viable
  local support rather than silently making nests portable.
- [x] **Biology vs identity:** humans and Moy'na are distinct people under normal
  bonds and Resurgence. Normal human separation is usually reversible, but can
  endanger the Moy'na. Convergence genuinely alters organism identity; its exact
  process is not selected yet. The threat is not presumed related to Moy'na/Krek.
- [x] **Resurgence and Ancestor access:** retain a Scion's existing authorization;
  permanent bonding prevents transferring the Ancestor gene to another person.
  Do not generalize that access to every bonded host or infer new transfer Recipes.
- [x] **Convergence and contamination:** authorization may remain functional while
  biological presence triggers escalating contamination responses. Warnings,
  quarantine and shutdown are direction, not authored numeric density thresholds.
  Convergent gene inheritance/transfer remains unresolved.
- [x] **Protection and irreplaceable assets:** CLP can treat Ancient equipment as
  expendable military assets, but motives do not bypass custody, capability,
  authored transformation or salvage/mass accounting.
- [x] **Protection and Moy'na:** military biological participation is a possible
  program direction; legal rights, rank, compensation and voluntary participation
  must not be replaced by an equipment-only treatment of sapient people. Do not
  silently authorize coercive procedures from the military theme.
- [x] **Survival ethic vs no conflict:** treatment, prisoner exchange, trade and
  preservation of infrastructure are broad norms, not automatic immunity from
  war, sabotage, espionage or coercion. Survival never guarantees recruitment.
- [x] **Ancestors vs active faction:** historical only. Promise, Chorus and Vigil
  recover historical systems, not living Ancients or a fourth major faction.
- [x] **CLP origin:** furniture-subscription business named from founder initials,
  gradually accumulating infrastructure and government functions. Choice,
  Logistics and Protection are later cultural interpretations, not a required
  original expansion of the acronym. Founder identity and formal reinterpretation
  remain unspecified.
- [x] **Historical failure:** effective planetary clearance and later reinfection
  are compatible. Lunar/off-world reservoirs remain a strong explanation, not
  confirmed history or an authored off-world travel mechanism.
