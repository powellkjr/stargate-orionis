# Profession Boundaries

## Status

**ACTIVE PROFESSION CONTRACT**

Use this document as the short authority for deciding which Profession owns a method.

Profession Curriculum provides trained method. Subject Theory provides domain knowledge. No Profession tier substitutes for missing Theory, Tool Services, Room Services, resources, authority, evidence, or valid state.

## Primary Ownership

| Profession | Owns | Does not inherently own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technician | Repairing, adapting, integrating, and implementing physical systems from known principles and requirements | Discovering unknown scientific truth |
| Scientist | Observing, testing, explaining, and generalizing evidence into scientific findings and requirements | Designing or building the physical implementation |
| Medic | Assessing, stabilizing, diagnosing, treating, and managing patient recovery | Generalizing unknown biology into Theory or resolving the environmental cause |
| Scout | Observing terrain, navigating, searching, tracking, predicting movement, and building reconnaissance pictures | Revealing hidden truth or directing combat |
| Soldier | Fighting, protecting, maneuvering, controlling engagements, and defining tactical requirements | Producing reconnaissance truth or resolving political legitimacy |
| Diplomat | Communicating, influencing, negotiating, representing, and structuring agreements and alignments | Mind control, hidden-intent revelation, or physical enforcement |

## Pairwise Boundaries

### Technician and Scientist

Scientist determines what is true and what conditions are required. Technician determines how to physically realize those known conditions.

### Technician and Medic

Medic defines patient needs and Clinical Care Requirements. Technician implements or restores the equipment and environment needed to satisfy them.

### Technician and Scout

Scout identifies field conditions, routes, clues, and operational needs. Technician evaluates, repairs, adapts, or implements the relevant physical systems.

### Technician and Soldier

Soldier defines the tactical need and operates in the engagement. Technician supports the physical capability without becoming the tactical decision-maker or demolitions specialist.

### Technician and Diplomat

Diplomat establishes authorized commitments and negotiated requirements. Technician determines whether and how their physical provisions can be implemented.

### Scientist and Medic

Medic establishes patient-level clinical findings and care. Scientist generalizes evidence into broader biological or medical knowledge; Research remains responsible for creating Theory.

### Scientist and Scout

Scout gathers and contextualizes field evidence. Scientist tests explanations and establishes justified models without retroactively granting the Scout hidden knowledge.

### Scientist and Soldier

Soldier may recognize and respond to threats without understanding their underlying mechanism. Scientist explains the phenomenon but does not direct the engagement.

### Scientist and Diplomat

Scientist establishes evidence-based facts and uncertainty. Diplomat decides how those facts are communicated, framed, negotiated, or represented without changing their truth.

### Medic and Scout

Scout locates patients, hazards, routes, and contextual evidence. Medic determines clinical significance and patient care.

### Medic and Soldier

Soldier secures the situation and addresses active hostile threats. Medic triages and treats patients; resolving the threat does not resolve patient consequences.

### Medic and Diplomat

Medic establishes clinical needs and constraints. Diplomat negotiates access, consent, resources, or commitments without overriding medical reality.

### Scout and Soldier

Scout develops the operational picture and predicts likely movement. Soldier converts that information into tactical priorities, maneuver, engagement, or disengagement.

### Scout and Diplomat

Scout gathers observable social and environmental context. Diplomat interprets interests, authority, commitments, and negotiation possibilities without treating inference as truth.

### Soldier and Diplomat

Soldier manages physical threat and tactical force. Diplomat manages communication, de-escalation, agreements, and legitimacy; neither automatically performs the other's resolution.

## Shared Handoff Rule

A handoff passes a finding, Requirement, plan, constraint, or state to another Profession. It does not transfer competency.

```text
Profession A output
+ Profession B competency
+ required Theory and Services
→ valid Profession B action
```

Tier III remains senior base-Profession competency. Exceptional methods remain specialization territory.
