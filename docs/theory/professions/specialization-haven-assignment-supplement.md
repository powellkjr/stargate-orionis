# Specialization Haven Assignment Supplement

## Purpose and Authority

These are fixed story-origin assignments for the 18 specialization curriculum
stories. Use this supplement with the
[Specializations and Haven Context Handoff](specializations-and-haven-context-handoff.md)
and [decision checklist](specializations-and-haven-decisions.md).

The latest confirmed names and progression rules in that handoff take precedence
over older terminology in the supplied assignment material. Detailed base
Profession curricula and the World Map population/economy specification retain
their existing authority. This supplement assigns origins, not capabilities.

The referenced **Profession Specialization Story Handoff** has not been located
in the repository. Recover or provide it before treating its additional story
requirements as documented rules; the assignments below are nevertheless
confirmed inputs and must not be reassigned during story authoring.

## Story and Curriculum Boundaries

An assignment identifies where the authored specialist story originates. It
does not mean that every practitioner belongs to that faction, that the faction
exclusively owns the specialization, or that every affiliated Haven practices it.
The SGC may subsequently teach the formal curriculum to other populations.

These stories concern how the SGC recognizes and formalizes locally held
expertise into a teachable curriculum. Canonical curriculum definitions must
still be authored before play: story progress may expose or establish SGC
Knowledge of those definitions, not procedurally invent canonical science,
curricula, Services or Recipes. This institutional story is distinct from a
Unit's supervised specialization onboarding and certification.

Individual faction-affiliated Havens and NPCs remain to be recovered or authored
where not already documented. Do not assume all six Scion stories share one
Haven, or silently choose a new Haven where a specific assignment already exists.

Independent Havens A, B and C are three separate, unrelated Havens, not a shared
Independent faction or culture. Their identities are not yet designed; A/B/C are
assignment placeholders, not canonical names or bindings to similarly named
population naming pools. These stories can introduce distinct civilizations.
