## Story rationale retained for specialization authoring

These explanations supplement, not replace, the fixed origin assignments.

- **Scientist:** Applied/Concord turns established science into interventions;
  Operational/CLP studies facility and organizational reliability;
  Strategic/Scions studies planetary, network and long-term inherited systems.
- **Diplomat:** Negotiator/Independent A survives by bargaining among stronger
  powers; Ambassador/Scions represents cultural identity and inheritance claims;
  Arbiter/CLP develops from contracts, ownership, liability and regulation.
- **Soldier:** Marksman/Concord emphasizes precise engagement around vulnerable
  living targets; Guardian/Scions protects people and irreplaceable assets;
  Tactician/CLP coordinates the group, engagement and maneuver.
- **Scout:** Pathfinder/Independent B arises from unreliable conventional routes;
  Tracker/Independent C from trails, movement and environmental signs;
  Observer/Scions understands sensitive inherited sites before intervention.
- **Technician:** Demolitions/Concord originates in precision expansion of
  occupied Moy'na nests: **subtractive engineering**, preserving surrounding
  biological spaces and infrastructure, not simply explosives expertise.
  Integrations/Scions connects inherited and newer systems; Overdrive/CLP accepts
  deliberate risk to exceed a validated envelope, distinct from ordinary TE3.
- **Medic:** Trauma/Scions draws on sophisticated inherited intervention;
  Field Medicine/Concord applies advanced care under field constraints;
  Epidemiology/CLP is medicine at population scale, with surveillance, logistics,
  quarantine and coordinated response.

Scion through-line: preserve, understand, protect and operate inherited systems.
CLP: organize, regulate, optimize and accept calculated risk.
Concord: apply precise expertise to living systems, including nest engineering.
Independent Havens: their distinct environments demanded their specializations.
