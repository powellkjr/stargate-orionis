# Technician v01 color study

`source.png` is an unchanged copy of the user's supplied
`orionis-technician-b001/technician__v01__source.png`. It is retained separately
from installable candidates; this study does not activate production art.

Provisional source-specific polygon masks and material color gates live in
`demos/sprite-viewer/character-colors.mjs`. Shirt, trousers, accents, hair and
skin, harness and gear/boots are independently adjustable. Dark outlines, transparent pixels and pixels
outside the reviewed regions remain unchanged. The chest display stays unchanged;
gear and harness now have their own controls. Masks are source-specific;
use Show masks to review coverage before extending them.

These regions fit this source only. Independently generated v02/v03 art needs
its own masks. Interchangeable heads require registered layers and a body with
the covered head area reconstructed; those layers do not exist in this study.
