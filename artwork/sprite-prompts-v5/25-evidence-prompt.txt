## Image Generation and Packaging Workflow

Generate one reference image containing exactly three variations of the requested asset, arranged horizontally in three clearly separated columns.

Each variation must:
- Follow the locked Stargate Orionis art direction.
- Show one complete subject, with nothing clipped.
- Use exactly the same overhead camera, lighting direction, scale, and footprint.
- Have a clear gap separating it from neighboring variations.
- Use a transparent background.
- Use the assigned v01/v02/v03 shape/design style; palette swaps and tiny surface-detail changes do not count as distinct variants.

The generated image is an intermediate production sheet, not the delivered asset.

After generation:
1. Extract each variation into a separate image.
2. Remove the surrounding background, preserving true RGBA transparency.
3. Center each subject on its own 1024×1024 transparent canvas.
4. Preserve proportions without stretching.
5. Validate transparency, padding, dimensions, and complete silhouettes.
6. Save as `evidence__v01__source.png`, `evidence__v02__source.png`, `evidence__v03__source.png`.
7. Package the three PNGs in `orionis-evidence-bNNN.zip` under a root folder matching the ZIP filename without `.zip` (use the batch number specified below).
8. Verify the ZIP and provide a clickable download link.

Do not include the intermediate sheet, previews, or any additional files in the archive.

For seamless floors and registered state pairs, use their asset-specific production workflows instead.

BATCH PACKAGING: Produce exactly three visual variants v01, v02, v03.
ZIP FILENAME: orionis-evidence-b001.zip (increment b002, b003 for later batches)
ZIP ROOT FOLDER: orionis-evidence-b001/
SOURCE FILENAMES: evidence__v01__source.png, evidence__v02__source.png, evidence__v03__source.png
VARIATION: Follow the three shape/design style briefs below, not colorway swaps. Preserve camera, subject scale, pivot, lighting and occupied footprint envelope. State pairs must match within each variant. No gameplay/identity changes.
ARCHIVE CONTENTS: PNG source files only inside the named root; no previews, contact sheets, nested folders or extra files. Attach an actual ZIP, not an image of filenames.
If ZIP/file export is unavailable, say so and deliver separate PNG files with these exact names; do not claim an archive exists.

ASSET ID: evidence
OUTPUT FILENAME: evidence__vNN__source.png (NN=01,02,03)
SOURCE ART SIZE: 1024x1024 PNG, single frame
RUNTIME FILENAME: evidence.png
RUNTIME SIZE: 128x128 pixels; 24x24 SVG world units
BACKGROUND: True RGBA transparency; no baked backdrop
CAMERA: Direct overhead, north up; no isometric or front-facing surfaces
ORIENTATION: North-facing neutral overhead
VISIBLE FOOTPRINT: One isolated subject, preserve aspect ratio; tool centers its alpha bounds
PIVOT: [0.5, 0.5], canvas center
PADDING: 8px minimum transparent runtime border; normalizer supplies border for isolated sprites
SHADOW: Tight centered translucent contact shadow; no directional cast shadow or backdrop
LIGHTING: Soft upper-left, low contrast; walls/doors/characters must tolerate 90-degree rotation
ROTATION POLICY: No directional variants; fixed orientation
MATCHING ASSET: None
ART DIRECTION: Bright clean chunky mature stylized 2D sci-fi, simplified painted materials, low noise, controlled cyan/amber emissives
SPECIAL REQUIREMENTS: No UI, labels, scripts or hidden technology/identity cues
SUBJECT DETAILS: One neutral closed document/evidence container viewed directly overhead; no exposed contents, readable labels or story clues.
VARIANT DESIGN BRIEF — three different shape/design styles, NOT three colorways:
v01 — Clean angular design: broad flat panels, crisp chamfers, squared corners and a simple rectilinear organization.
v02 — Soft rounded design: curved shell/panel boundaries, rounded corners and a flowing organization of the same visible components.
v03 — Chunky utilitarian design: stepped contours, layered panel construction and a robust block-like organization of the same visible components.
DESIGN DIFFERENCE: change at least three major visible shape features between each pair of variants, such as contour treatment, panel/seam layout and component or garment shape. These must read at runtime size, not only as tiny texture details. Do not duplicate a design and recolor it. Keep the same palette across all three; color changes do not count as variation.
LOCKED PRESENTATION: same bright clean chunky mature stylized painted sci-fi rendering, camera, orientation, lighting, subject scale, occupied footprint envelope and pivot. Different design styles are NOT different rendering media: no pixel-art/anime/photorealistic mixture. Contours and internal geometry may differ within the required envelope; do not enlarge one variant to make it look different.
SEMANTIC LIMITS: preserve the exact public subject, required components, equipment inventory and purpose. Do not add/remove Tools, change capability or tier, imply a new faction/civilization, expose hidden identity, or turn surface appearance into a new game rule. Subject-specific prohibitions take priority over design suggestions; do not add panels to a natural subject or decoration to an empty/void floor.
VARIATION CHECK: inspect v01/v02/v03 side by side at runtime dimensions and in grayscale. Each should be distinguishable by major shape structure without color cues. Reject colorway-only duplicates and regenerate before packaging. If the subject constraints prevent meaningful differences, report the limitation rather than inventing new content.
